## The idea, in one sentence
Late at night, a young man sits on the floor against the side of his bed, head bowed over a
half-full glass of something dark, and the small lamp beside him is the only thing in the room
the ink has not swallowed.

- **Focal subject** (mine): the hands round the glass and the bowed head above them, the two
  places the lamp lands hardest.
- **Light** (mine): a small shaded lamp on a nightstand at the left. In sumi-e, light is paper
  left bare: a pool of untouched xuan round the lamp, and the room built of wet washes that
  darken with distance from it. His back, the far side of the bed and the right corner are the
  deepest ink.
- **Pose** (mine): seated on the floor, back against the mattress, knees drawn up, forearms on
  the knees, both hands holding the glass low between the shins, head hung forward over it.
- **Reference** (from knowledge, mine): Liang Kai's splashed-ink figures and Xu Beihong's ink
  figures for the economy (a fine line for face and hands, broad wet strokes for clothing);
  adult seated canon of 7.5 heads (Loomis); the tumbler at life size against the head (about 13 cm
  against 23 cm).
- **Seal** (mine): cinnabar, lower right, an invented glyph, deliberately not text.
